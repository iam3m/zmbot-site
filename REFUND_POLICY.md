# ZMBOT — Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-05-27
**Applies to:** All purchases of ZMBOT Cloud and ZMBOT Standalone made on or after the effective date above. Purchases made before this date are governed by the prior marketing-page guarantee, subject to the same fair-use clauses below.

This is the canonical refund policy for ZMBOT. The customer-facing page lives at https://zm-bot.com/refund-policy and is the version buyers agree to at checkout.

---

## 1. Standalone version — one-time purchase via Gumroad ($249)

You may request a full refund within **30 days** of purchase by emailing zoran@miljak.me with your Gumroad order number.

Refunds will be issued in good faith, except in the following cases:

- The customer has previously received a refund for any ZMBOT product (Standalone or Cloud).
- The license has been used to execute more than 200 trades total (live or dry-run combined).
- Multiple licenses have been purchased by the same customer to evade the single-refund rule.
- The customer has been refunded already and is requesting a refund on a second purchase.

After 30 days, all sales are final.

## 2. Cloud version — subscription via Stripe (from $119/month)

**Cancellation is always free.** Cancel via your customer portal or by messaging us. Future billing stops immediately. You retain access through the end of your already-paid period.

**14-day money-back guarantee.** Within 14 days of a successful Stripe charge, you may request a full refund of that charge by emailing zoran@miljak.me. Refunds are issued in good faith.

We reserve the right to decline a Cloud refund where one or more of the following fair-use conditions apply:

- The customer has previously received a refund for any ZMBOT product (Standalone or Cloud) — see Section 4.
- The customer has switched the bot from dry-run to live trading AND executed at least one real fill on Hyperliquid (i.e. the software has been used for its intended purpose with real capital).
- The customer has generated more than 200 trades total (live and dry-run combined).
- The customer has filed a chargeback with their card provider — see Section 5.
- Multiple subscriptions have been purchased by the same customer to evade the one-refund rule.

After 14 days, refunds of past charges are not available. Cancellation stops future charges from the next billing cycle.

## 3. Definition of "use"

For both tiers, the following actions count as using the product:

- Downloading and activating the standalone Gumroad license (any call to the license-verify endpoint counts as activation).
- Completing the `/setup` configuration on the cloud bot — including connecting your Hyperliquid wallet, entering an API key, or setting `bet_size`.
- Generating any trade signals, simulated trades, or live trades — **including in dry-run / paper-trading mode**.
- Accessing the customer dashboard after the welcome email has been sent.

**Dry-run mode is a fully-supported, paid feature of ZMBOT, not a free trial.** Brief evaluation in dry-run mode is fine and expected — the per-tier trade-count thresholds in Sections 1 and 2 (200 trades) define when usage becomes heavy enough to disqualify a refund.

## 4. One refund per customer

A customer who has received a refund for any ZMBOT product (Standalone or Cloud) is **not eligible** for a refund on any subsequent purchase. Repeat purchases by previously-refunded customers are sold "as-is" and are final.

We reserve the right to decline service to customers showing a pattern of purchase-then-refund behavior.

---

## 5. Chargebacks

If a refund is denied per this policy and the customer files a chargeback with their card provider:

- The customer is permanently banned from purchasing or accessing any current or future ZMBOT product or service.
- The disputed charge will be defended with full evidence: configuration logs, trade history, dashboard usage, Hyperliquid wallet activity, and timestamps of customer-side actions.
- Recurring access (cloud bot, license keys) is terminated immediately on dispute filing.

## 6. Discretionary refunds

We may, at our sole discretion, grant refunds outside this policy in cases of:

- Confirmed technical failure on our side that prevented the customer from using the product.
- Serious personal hardship, on a case-by-case basis.

To request a discretionary refund, email zoran@miljak.me with full details and supporting context. There is no entitlement to a discretionary refund.
